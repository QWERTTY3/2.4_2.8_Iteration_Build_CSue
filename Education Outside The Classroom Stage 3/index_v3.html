ABOUT

Year 10 E.O.T.C


As part of the Year 10 programme students
will be participating in four days of
Education Outside the Classroom (EOTC).
The focus of this programme is one of
citizenship and social awareness.

OUTDOOR ACTIVITIES

What we do?


The four days of activities in which your son/daughter
will participate are as follows: Community Service,
Rangituhi Walk, Adrenaline Forest/Te Rauparaha
Arena Aquatic Centre and Wainuiomata Camp.

COMMUNITY SERVICE

How is this done?


Students will complete one day of
community service assisting local residents
and businesses. This will usually occur
within walking distance of Tawa College
for all Year 10 students. This day has the key
focus of citizenship and has been a great
success in previous years.
